# Lesson 3: Filing your tax return
<!-- facts: Filing season -->

## By the end, the learner can

- Find out whether they need to file, using SARS's own check.
- Explain auto-assessment and what to do if they disagree with one.
- Name the filing season dates for their situation (from `facts.md`).
- Describe the steps of filing on eFiling or the MobiApp.

## Key ideas

**Filing season** is when SARS accepts returns (the ITR12) for the tax year that just ended.
Dates differ for auto-assessed people, non-provisional and provisional taxpayers; see `facts.md`.

**Do you need to file?** Several rules decide it. Use SARS's own check, linked in `facts.md`,
rather than a rule of thumb.

**Auto-assessment.** SARS fills in your return from what employers, banks, medical schemes and
retirement funds reported, and sends you an assessment. If it's right, you do nothing. If
something is missing (a retirement annuity you paid privately, extra income), you file a
corrected return before the deadline.

**Steps to file:**
1. Register on eFiling or the MobiApp, if you haven't (you need your ID and a tax number;
   SARS registers you automatically when you start formal work).
2. Open the return for the right year. Check the pre-filled figures against your IRP5 and other
   certificates.
3. Add anything missing, with supporting documents ready in case SARS asks.
4. Submit, then check the assessment: refund, nothing owed, or amount due and its due date.

**Keep records for five years.** SARS can ask for proof.

**Late returns** can attract monthly penalties. Filing late is better than not filing.

## Exercise

Ask the learner which filing season dates apply to them, and what they'd check before accepting
an auto-assessment.
