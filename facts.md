## Retirement fund contributions

Source: [Budget 2026 FAQ](https://www.sars.gov.za/about/sars-tax-and-customs-system/budget/budget-2026-frequently-asked-questions/), checked 2026-11-02.

Contributions to pension, provident and retirement annuity funds are deductible up to 27.5% of the
greater of remuneration or taxable income, capped at R430 000 a year.

## Tax-free savings accounts

Source: [Tax free investments](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/tax-free-investments/), checked 2026-11-02.

- Annual limit: R46 000 (from 1 March 2026), across all your tax-free accounts together.
- Lifetime limit: R500 000.
- Going over either limit triggers a penalty tax on the excess.
